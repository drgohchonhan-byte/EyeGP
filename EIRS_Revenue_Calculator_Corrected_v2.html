🧠 How Is This Calculated?

EIRS Reports / Month
= Patients / Month × EIRS Adoption %

= EIRS Reports / Month × Consultation + EIRS Fee

= EIRS Reports / Month × EIRS Cost / Report

= Consultation + EIRS Revenue
